Hermes Personal Assistant

A private integration operated by MarTyNiDruid for the account owner's personal use. It connects the owner's Google account to their own Hermes Agent installation. It is not a public sign-up service or an official Google product.

Purpose and permissions

The integration helps its owner read and summarize Gmail messages, track personal commitments, and read and manage Google Calendar events. Authorized Gmail permissions also allow sending messages and updating message labels or read state. Actions are subject to the owner's instructions and authorizations.

Google access is granted through OAuth. This public information website does not sign users in, request Google permissions, or display private account data. Relevant email or calendar content may be sent by the assistant to its configured AI model provider to perform the owner's requested tasks.

Privacy policy · Terms of use

Terms of use

This integration is for its owner's personal use only and is provided as-is. It is not offered to other users. The owner controls the connected account, authorizes assistant actions, reviews important outputs, and can stop using the integration or revoke Google access at any time.
