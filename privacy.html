Hermes Personal Assistant

Privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Google integration operated by MarTyNiDruid. It is used only by its owner and is not a public application offered to other users.

Google account data and purpose

With the owner's OAuth consent, the integration can access Gmail messages and metadata and Google Calendar events. Its authorized permissions allow reading email, sending email, modifying Gmail message state or labels, and reading, creating, updating, or deleting calendar events. These capabilities support the owner's personal assistance, summaries, reminders, correspondence, and scheduling. The owner determines which actions are permitted.

Processing and sharing

Hermes runs on the owner's computer and uses Google APIs. Relevant message or event content may be sent to the configured AI model provider to carry out the owner's tasks. Provider-side processing and retention depend on the selected service's terms and account settings. The integration does not sell Google account data, provide it to advertising services, or use it to train its own generalized AI models. This is not a claim that all processing remains on the owner's computer.

Storage, security, and retention

OAuth credentials are kept in the owner's local assistant environment, not in this public website or repository. Task-relevant information may remain in local chat history, notes, logs, or assistant memory until the owner removes it. Local records may be included in the owner's backups and synchronized to the owner's cloud backup storage. Copies in backups follow the owner's backup retention arrangements. This policy does not promise immediate deletion from every backup or from an AI provider's systems.

Control, revocation, and deletion

The owner can stop the integration and revoke its Google permissions in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not itself erase previously stored local records. The owner controls deletion of local credentials, records, memories, and backups, and any provider-side deletion options available under the provider's terms.

This website

This static website contains only public information. It has no Google sign-in, account-data upload, application cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels. GitHub Pages hosts it; hosting access logs and other infrastructure processing are governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Questions or corrections can be submitted through the website repository's issues. Do not include private messages, calendar details, tokens, or other credentials in a public issue. Material changes to this personal integration will be reflected on this page.
